# Portrait framing rules

## Lens / focal length per framing

| Framing | Equivalent focal length | Distance |
|---|---|---|
| Tight close-up (face only) | 85–135mm | 1.5–2.5m |
| Close-up (head + shoulders) | 50–85mm | 1.5–2m |
| Medium (head to waist) | 35–50mm | 2–3m |
| Wide (full body) | 24–35mm | 3–5m |
| Environmental portrait (subject in space) | 18–35mm | 4m+ |

Wider lenses on close-ups distort facial proportions. Avoid 16–18mm for any tight portrait.

## Eye-line placement

- Tight close-up: eyes upper third (rule of thirds top line)
- Close-up: eyes upper third
- Medium / wide: eyes upper third or upper half
- Environmental: eyes anywhere; subject placement matters more

## Subject placement

| Mood | Placement |
|---|---|
| Confident hero (centered or right-third for negative space CTA) | Right-third |
| Editorial documentary | Off-center, gaze leads into negative space |
| Symmetric brand portrait | Centered |
| Cinematic narrative | Variable; depth cues drive placement |

## Aspect-specific notes

- 16:9: room for headline / CTA on opposite side of subject; use right-third.
- 1:1: subject fills frame; minimal negative space.
- 9:16: subject upper third; lower third reserved for caption / CTA.
- 2.39:1: subject off-center; horizontal negative space tells story.
