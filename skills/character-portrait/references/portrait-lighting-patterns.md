# Portrait lighting patterns

## Standard 3-point (default for hero / editorial)

- **Key**: 50–500W area light, 45° from camera, slightly above subject eye-line. Soft size 0.4–1.0m for soft hero; 0.1–0.3m for harder editorial.
- **Fill**: 10–20% of key strength, opposite side of subject, larger soft size.
- **Rim**: 50–100W spot or area, behind subject 135° from camera, separates subject from background.

## Rembrandt (editorial / dramatic)

- Key at 45° creating triangle of light on shadow-side cheek.
- Minimal fill; shadow-side is darker.
- Rim optional.
- Mood: classic, painterly, contemplative.

## Butterfly (beauty / glamour)

- Key directly in front of subject, slightly above; soft.
- Fill from below to lift shadows under chin.
- Rim optional.
- Mood: glamorous, polished.

## Split (dramatic / mystery)

- Key 90° to one side; one half of face lit, other half in shadow.
- Minimal fill.
- Mood: noir, character study.

## Loop (versatile portrait)

- Key just off-center, slightly above; small loop shadow from nose.
- Fill subordinate.
- Rim optional.
- Mood: friendly, accessible.

## HDRI use in portraits

- Studio HDRI 0.5–1.0 for ambient + ground reflection.
- Outdoor HDRI 0.7–1.5 for environmental context.
- Avoid HDRI alone for hero portraits — explicit key light gives the snap that HDRI alone lacks.

## Background

- Solid color seamless: cleanest hero look.
- Gradient: subtle depth.
- Studio backdrop with subtle texture: editorial.
- Environmental: storytelling but ensure subject still reads.
