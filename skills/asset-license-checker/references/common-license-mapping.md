# Common license → permissions mapping

| License | Commercial | Modification | Redistribution | Attribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CC0 / Public Domain | Y | Y | Y | N (recommended but not required) |
| CC-BY 4.0 | Y | Y | Y | Y (required, specific format) |
| CC-BY-SA 4.0 | Y | Y | Y (under same license) | Y |
| CC-BY-NC 4.0 | N | Y | Y (non-commercial only) | Y |
| CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0 | N | Y | Y (non-commercial, same license) | Y |
| CC-BY-ND 4.0 | Y | N (no derivatives) | Y | Y |
| MIT | Y | Y | Y | Y (license text) |
| Apache 2.0 | Y | Y | Y | Y |
| GPL v3 | Y | Y | Y (under GPL) | Y |
| LGPL | Y | Y (library use) | Y | Y |
| Proprietary / EULA | Read EULA | Read EULA | Read EULA | Read EULA |
| All Rights Reserved | N (without explicit grant) | N | N | N/A |
| "Free for personal use" | **N (commercial blocked)** | Read terms | Read terms | Read terms |

## Pitfalls

- "Free" does not mean "free for any use". Always read the specific terms.
- "Free for personal use" excludes commercial; this is the most common mismatch.
- CC-NC variants exclude commercial. Brand campaigns are commercial.
- "Royalty-free" usually means "you can use without paying again per use", not "free of any license".
