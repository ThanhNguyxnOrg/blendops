# Hero Composition Patterns

## Purpose
Provide concrete, repeatable hero framing and camera setups matched to common product, e-commerce, and web-landing delivery goals.

## When to use
- During `camera-composition` planning immediately after the hero subject footprint (`dimensions`, facing direction) is known.

## Core Hero Composition Patterns

| Pattern | Camera Geometry & Focal Range | Best For | Key Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Center-Dominant Hero** | `65-85 mm`, `10-15 deg` slightly above eye-level (`Z + 0.15 m`), straight-on or `15 deg` yaw | Luxury packaging, sneaker side/3-4 profile, standalone hero turntable | Leave `15%+` top/bottom margin so shadow and badge badges never touch frame edge |
| **Rule-of-Thirds Split (Web Landing)** | `50-70 mm`, product placed on left or right vertical third (`shift_x` or aim offset), hero occupies `40%-55%` frame | SaaS/marketing hero banners with headline + CTA on the opposite side | Verify `40%+` opposite-side uncluttered background and test desktop `16:9` -> mobile `4:5` safe crop |
| **Three-Quarter Dynamic Diagonal** | `50-65 mm`, `35-45 deg` yaw + subtle `12-20 deg` low-to-high pitch (`+3-5 deg` subtle roll only if energetic tech/sport) | Footwear, hardware electronics, wearable gadgets needing depth + sole/back detail | Confirm diagonal leading lines point **into** the frame rather than exiting a corner |
| **Macro Craft Detail Crop** | `85-105 mm` telephoto, tight partial crop on stitching, chamfer, metallic bevel, or brushed anodizing (`f/4-f/5.6`) | Secondary gallery shots, material credibility cards, craftsmanship proof | Only pair with a wider establishing shot; never use macro as the sole hero shot |
| **Elevated Isometric / Orthographic Grid** | `85-135 mm` long lens (or true ortho `ortho_scale`), `30-35 deg` elevation, `45 deg` yaw | Product catalog consistency, multi-SKU comparisons, configurator thumbnails | Lock exact camera distance & focal preset across every SKU in the batch |

## Good vs. Bad Selection
- **Good**: `"Using Rule-of-Thirds Split (60mm, hero shifted right, left 45% dark matte gradient reserved for landing H1 + CTA), 12% margin above heel cap."`
- **Bad**: Picking a low-angle `24 mm` Dutch tilt for a clean minimalist skincare e-commerce thumbnail where straight verticals and multi-SKU consistency are required.
