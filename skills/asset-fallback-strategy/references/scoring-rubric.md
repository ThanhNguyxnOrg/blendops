# Scoring rubric for fallback options

Score each option on 4 dimensions; recommend the best total.

## Dimensions

### 1. Time to implement

- < 1 hour: 5
- 1-4 hours: 4
- 4-8 hours: 3
- 1-3 days: 2
- > 3 days: 1

### 2. Brief match

- Exact match: 5
- Very close: 4
- Close (minor caveat): 3
- Approximate: 2
- Loose: 1

### 3. License confidence

- CC0 / MIT / fully cleared: 5
- CC-BY with clear attribution plan: 4
- Paid commercial with verified terms: 4
- Procedurally generated by you: 5
- Generative AI with clear terms: 3
- Generative AI with unclear terms: 1
- Unknown: 1

### 4. Quality match (per scene quality bar)

- Exceeds bar: 5
- Meets bar: 4
- Slightly below bar: 3
- Notably below: 2
- Unusable: 1

## Aggregation

- Sum the 4 scores; max 20.
- Recommend highest total.
- Tie-break: prefer lower time + higher license confidence.

## Examples

Option (1) CC0 from polyhaven: time 5, brief 3, license 5, quality 4 → 17.
Option (2) Paid $99 commercial: time 3, brief 5, license 4, quality 5 → 17.
Option (3) Procedural Geometry Nodes: time 1, brief 4, license 5, quality 4 → 14.

Tied (1) and (2) — pick (2) for brief fidelity if the quality bar matters most.

## When score is low

- Highest total < 12 → no good fallback exists.
- Recommend recipe scope reduction or wait-for-original.
