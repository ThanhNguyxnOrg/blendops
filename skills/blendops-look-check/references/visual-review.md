# Visual review

Look at the pictures only after the numeric checks, and only for what numbers cannot tell: whether it reads as the right thing and looks good.

Compare each picture against the brief, the reference images if any, and the look criteria from `blendops-done-criteria`.

| Area | Ask |
|---|---|
| Shape | Does the silhouette read as the planned object from every view? Any missing or extra parts? |
| Proportions | Do parts look the right size relative to each other? Confirm with the numeric size check, not by eye. |
| Materials | Does each surface read as the planned material (metal, rubber, glass)? Any pink (missing texture) or plain grey surfaces? |
| Lighting | Is the subject lit? Any blown-out white or crushed black areas, harsh shadows the brief did not ask for? |
| Framing | Is the subject where the brief wants it, with the planned margin, not cut off? |
| Background | Is the background the planned colour or setting, with nothing stray in shot? |

Rules:

- Write each issue as one line: area, what is wrong, which view shows it, how bad (`fail` blocks the brief, `warn` is a polish item).
- Rotation, mirrored parts and exact sizes are the weak spot of vision models: check them with numbers or a second view before reporting.
- When you are unsure an issue is real, say so and keep it a `warn`.
- Do not judge final render quality (noise, samples) on a small preview.
