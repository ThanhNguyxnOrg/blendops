# Furniture layout rules

## Living room baseline

- Sofa facing focal point (fireplace / TV / window view).
- Coffee table 40–50cm from sofa, height matching sofa seat.
- Area rug under or anchoring the seating area.
- Optional armchair + side table + floor lamp.
- Negative space behind / around furniture for camera angles.

## Bedroom baseline

- Bed centered against one wall (head against wall, not under window).
- Bedside tables symmetric.
- Wardrobe along longer wall.
- Optional reading chair + lamp.

## Office baseline

- Desk facing into room (back to wall) or facing window.
- Chair clearance 80cm behind desk.
- Bookshelf or storage along long wall.
- Single accent chair for visitors.

## Kitchen baseline

- Triangle: stove / sink / fridge within reachable distance.
- Counter depth 60cm; clearance 90–120cm.
- Island optional; requires 1.2m+ clearance all sides.

## Hospitality lobby baseline

- Reception / focal art on focal wall.
- Seating clusters (2-3 sofas + armchair + table).
- Path of travel clear from entrance to elevators / desk.

## Camera-friendly arrangement

- Place "hero furniture piece" (sofa, dining table, bed) so the camera can frame it from a corner.
- Avoid blocking camera path with door or low-ceiling beam.
- Foreground prop (lamp / armchair) gives depth.

## Common failures

- Furniture floating in center of room (no anchor).
- Sofa pressed against window (blocks light).
- Multiple focal points fighting (TV + fireplace + view all visible).
- No path of travel between camera and back wall.
