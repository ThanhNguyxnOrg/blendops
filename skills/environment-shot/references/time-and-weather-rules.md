# Time of day + weather rules

## Time of day → light + sky

| Time | Sun azimuth | Sun strength | Sky tint | HDRI suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dawn | low east, ~10° | 0.5–1.0 | warm pink-orange | dawn HDRI |
| Mid-morning | ~30° | 1.5–2.5 | clear blue | sunny day |
| Noon | overhead, ~80° | 3–5 | bright blue | overcast or clear |
| Golden hour | low west, ~10° | 1–2 | deep orange-red | golden hour |
| Blue hour (post-sunset) | below horizon | 0 (no direct sun) | deep blue-purple | dusk |
| Night | below horizon | 0 | dark blue / black | night sky |
| Stylized "neon night" | below horizon | minor | mostly occluded | dark sky + practicals dominate |

## Weather → atmosphere + sky

| Weather | Sun visibility | Atmosphere density | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clear | direct | 0.0–0.02 | Sharp shadows |
| Partly cloudy | broken | 0.02–0.05 | Variable shadows |
| Overcast | diffuse | 0.05–0.1 | Soft shadows |
| Light rain | diffuse | 0.05–0.15 | Wet ground reflections |
| Heavy rain | diffuse / occluded | 0.1–0.3 | Reduced visibility |
| Fog | occluded | 0.3+ | Subject at distance lost |
| Snow | diffuse | varies | Ground albedo high |

## Common contradictions

- Sunny + noir mood → Fail; pick one.
- Heavy fog + sharp distant skyline → Fail (visibility contradicts).
- Dawn warm tint + cool blue mood → Warn unless intentional split.
- Stylized neon night + realistic sky HDRI → Fail; pick one paradigm.
