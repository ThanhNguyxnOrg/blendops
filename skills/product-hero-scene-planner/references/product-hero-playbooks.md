# Product Hero Playbooks

## Purpose
Offer distinct, campaign-ready visual playbooks so `product-hero-scene-planner` adapts staging, lighting ratio, floor behavior, and camera focal presets to the user's brand instead of forcing a dark neon aesthetic onto every product.

## When to use
- Immediately after `intent-to-3d-brief-writer` when choosing the visual direction for a product hero shot.

## Four Campaign Playbooks

| Playbook | Stage & Floor | Lighting Ratio & Color Mood | Camera Preset | Target Audience Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Dark Cyberpunk / Tech Hero** | Matte-satin dark acrylic (`#0A0E17`, `Roughness 0.16-0.25`) with subtle contact reflection | Dramatic `5:1` ratio; neutral soft top key + cyan (`#00E5FF`) / magenta (`#FF2A85`) rim accents; `AgX Medium High Contrast` | `60-75 mm`, dynamic 3/4 yaw (`35-45 deg`), optional `5 deg` hero hover | Footwear, gaming peripherals, audio gear, futuristic hardware |
| **2. Clean Studio / Apple-Style Minimal** | Seamless cyclorama infinity sweep (`#F3F4F6`, subtle soft shadow catcher, `Roughness 0.85`) | Soft `2:1` broad wraparound area cards + crisp specular edge strip (`5500K-6000K`); `Khronos PBR Neutral` | `70-85 mm`, clean 3/4 or orthographic-feel front elevation, zero roll | Consumer electronics, minimalist skincare, industrial hardware |
| **3. Minimal E-Commerce Catalog Card** | Pure clean neutral plinth or transparent shadow-catcher floor (`Z = 0` strict grounding) | Even `1.5:1` commercial illumination preserving exact label/stitching legibility; zero color cast | `85 mm` locked studio preset, `1:1` or `4:5` tight `60%` fill | Multi-SKU storefront thumbnails, Shopify/Amazon 3D viewers (`<= 3 MB` `.glb`) |
| **4. Warm Editorial / Craft Detail** | Natural travertine, oak, or brushed slate pedestal with soft physical rim gradient | Directional `3:1` golden window key (`3500K-4200K`) + soft warm bounce card (`f/4` shallow accents) | `85-105 mm`, low-angle hero or tactile 3/4 close-up | Watches, fragrance glass, ceramics, artisan goods |

## Selection Rule
- Match playbook strictly to **user audience + deliverable format**; never inject neon rims into a minimalist skincare or e-commerce catalog brief unless explicitly requested.
